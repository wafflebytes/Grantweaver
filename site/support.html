FAQ

Does Grantweaver read all our messages?

No. It searches only when you ask, through Slack's Real-Time Search API, which respects Slack's own permissions. Results are used to answer your request, then discarded. We store links to messages (permalinks), never the messages themselves.

What does the AI do with our data?

Your request plus live search results are processed by MiniMax 2.7 through an OpenAI-compatible API for the duration of the request; Grantweaver itself never stores Slack content, and we never train models on your data. Drafts always cite sources so you can verify every claim.

How do I see what Grantweaver did?

Run /grantweaver logs for recent runs, tool names, status, latency, model, and sanitized errors. Run /grantweaver state in a DM or thread to inspect the saved goal, artifact pointers, source pointer count, and summary.

How do I exclude a channel?

Run /grantweaver settings and choose channels under "AI excluded channels." Grantweaver will not search or scan excluded channels, and if mentioned there it will explain that AI is disabled for that channel.

How do I inspect or delete state?

Run /grantweaver state to inspect conversation state. Run /grantweaver reset to clear the workspace profile, pipeline, evidence pointers, watches, and index; Slack Canvases and Lists remain in Slack as orphaned artifacts.

Do we need a paid Slack plan?

No, Grantweaver works on all plans. Workspaces with Slack AI search get semantic search ("how did attendance change?"); other plans get smart keyword search automatically.

Which grants does it search?

All live federal opportunities on Grants.gov, updated in real time. Foundation grants are on our roadmap.

Does it submit applications?

Never. Grantweaver drafts; your team reviews and submits. Every draft says so.

How do we delete our data?

Uninstall the app (auto-deletes within 30 days) or email chaitanyajha.quantum@gmail.com any time.
