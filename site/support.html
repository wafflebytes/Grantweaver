FAQ

Does Grantweaver read all our messages?

No. It searches only when you ask, through Slack's Real-Time Search API, which respects Slack's own permissions. Results are used to answer your request, then discarded. We store links to messages (permalinks), never the messages themselves.

What does the AI do with our data?

Your request plus the search results are processed by our LLM provider for the duration of the request; Grantweaver itself never stores your content, and we never train models on your data. Drafts always cite sources so you can verify every claim.

Do we need a paid Slack plan?

No, Grantweaver works on all plans. Workspaces with Slack AI search get semantic search ("how did attendance change?"); other plans get smart keyword search automatically.

Which grants does it search?

All live federal opportunities on Grants.gov, updated in real time. Foundation grants are on our roadmap.

Does it submit applications?

Never. Grantweaver drafts; your team reviews and submits. Every draft says so.

How do we delete our data?

Uninstall the app (auto-deletes within 30 days) or email chaitanyajha.quantum@gmail.com any time.

Something's broken.

Email chaitanyajha.quantum@gmail.com and we'll get back to you within 2 business days, usually much faster.
