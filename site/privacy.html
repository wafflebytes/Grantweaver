Privacy Policy

v0.1 · July 2026

What we collect

(1) The organization profile you type into setup (name, mission, focus areas, state, team size). (2) Grant pipeline records you create (public Grants.gov opportunity metadata + stage). (3) Evidence pointers: Slack permalinks, channel IDs, message timestamps, and a tag — never message text, files, or attachments. (4) Feedback ratings on Grantweaver's responses. (5) Standard service logs (event types, timestamps — no message content).

What we never collect or store

Slack message content, file content, or user DMs. When Grantweaver reads workspace content it does so live via Slack's Real-Time Search API, uses it only to answer the current request, and discards it. We do not train AI models on your data, ever.

AI processing

Requests are processed by a third-party large-language-model API (provider and model disclosed in our Marketplace submission); content transits the provider only for the duration of your request and is never stored by Grantweaver. Provider-side handling follows that provider's API terms, linked from our submission. We never train models on your data ourselves. AI outputs may contain errors — always review drafts before submission.

Retention & deletion

Profile, pipeline, and pointer data persist until you delete them or uninstall the app; uninstalling schedules deletion within 30 days. Email privacy@grantweaver.app to access, export, or delete your data.

Contact & incidents

privacy@grantweaver.app · security issues: security@grantweaver.app. We notify affected workspaces of confirmed incidents without undue delay.
