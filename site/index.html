The loop

How it works

  1. 1

    Discover

    Ask for grants in plain English. Grantweaver searches live Grants.gov listings and scores each match against your mission, so you see the handful worth reading instead of five hundred that happen to share a keyword.

  2. 2

    Prove

    The proof funders want is already in your Slack: attendance numbers in #program-updates, a parent's thank-you in #stories, the attendance sheet someone uploaded in May. Setup scans the channels you choose and shows you an evidence index: what your workspace can already prove, by theme. From then on Grantweaver searches live with Slack's Real-Time Search, links every quote to the original message or file, and your team saves new evidence with a single 🧵 reaction.

  3. 3

    Draft

    One request turns that evidence into a proposal draft in a Slack Canvas, after you confirm on a card, never behind your back. Every figure is cited. Anything your workspace can't back up gets marked "team to confirm" rather than invented, and the whole team can request changes in a thread that Grantweaver applies in one pass, diff included.

  4. 4

    Track

    A pipeline board in the App Home and a real Slack List, synced both ways: drag a stage in the List and the agent knows. Deadline nudges before things slip, a check-in when a grant goes quiet, a requirements checklist per opportunity, and an impact meter that counts what the agent actually saved you. Plus a shareable evidence page for the board member who isn't in your Slack.

Privacy by architecture

Your workspace is read, never copied.

Grantweaver never stores your messages or files. It reads workspace content live through Slack's Real-Time Search API when you ask, keeps permalinks rather than content, and re-reads the sources at drafting time. Your data is never used to train any model.

What we store

  • The org profile you type into setup
  • Pipeline stages for grants you track
  • Links to messages you tag as evidence

What we never store

  • Message text or files
  • DMs or private conversations
  • Anything you didn't ask us to keep

About the AI: Grantweaver uses a large language model (provider disclosed in our submission and privacy policy). AI makes mistakes, so every draft cites its sources and waits for your review before anything goes out. Admins can exclude channels from AI entirely and audit every run from inside Slack. Semantic workspace search needs a Slack plan with AI search; keyword search works on every plan, including free.

Run it yourself: Grantweaver is open source (MIT). The GitHub repo has a 10-minute setup guide: a Slack app manifest, one Postgres database, and any OpenAI-compatible model key is all it takes. We deploy on Railway; one service hosts everything, including this site.
