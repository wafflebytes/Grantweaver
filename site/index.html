Privacy by architecture

Grantweaver never stores your messages or files. It reads workspace content live through Slack's Real-Time Search API only when you ask, keeps permalinks — not content — and re-reads sources at drafting time. No training on your data. Ever.

About the AI: Grantweaver uses a large language model (provider disclosed in our submission and privacy policy). AI can make mistakes — every draft cites its sources and asks for your review before anything is submitted. Semantic workspace search requires a Slack plan with AI search; smart keyword search works on all plans, including free.
