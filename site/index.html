The loop

How it works

  1. 1

    Discover

    Ask for grants in plain English. Grantweaver searches live Grants.gov listings and scores each match against your mission, so you see the handful worth reading instead of five hundred that happen to share a keyword.

  2. 2

    Prove

    The proof funders want is already in your Slack: attendance numbers in #program-updates, a parent's thank-you in #stories. Grantweaver finds it with Slack's Real-Time Search, quotes it verbatim, and links every quote to the original message.

  3. 3

    Draft

    One request turns that evidence into a proposal draft in a Slack Canvas. Every figure is cited. Anything your workspace can't back up gets marked "team to confirm" rather than invented.

  4. 4

    Track

    A pipeline board in the App Home, deadline nudges before things slip, and an impact meter that counts what the agent actually saved you: hours returned, dollars applied for.

Privacy by architecture

Your workspace is read, never copied.

Grantweaver never stores your messages or files. It reads workspace content live through Slack's Real-Time Search API when you ask, keeps permalinks rather than content, and re-reads the sources at drafting time. Your data is never used to train any model.

What we store

  • The org profile you type into setup
  • Pipeline stages for grants you track
  • Links to messages you tag as evidence

What we never store

  • Message text or files
  • DMs or private conversations
  • Anything you didn't ask us to keep

About the AI: Grantweaver uses a large language model (provider disclosed in our submission and privacy policy). AI makes mistakes, so every draft cites its sources and waits for your review before anything goes out. Semantic workspace search needs a Slack plan with AI search; keyword search works on every plan, including free.
